What we do

Six ways we
support your quality
operation

Whether your problem is machine downtime, slow cycle times, compliance paperwork, or a legacy programme nobody trusts — we have a service that addresses it directly.

01
Offline CAD Programming Zero machine downtime

We write and simulate CMM routines from your STEP or IGES files with full collision-check. Your machine keeps producing while we work. Delivered with virtual prove-out data.

02
On-Site Execution & Prove-Out UK & Ireland deployment

Our engineers embed with your team — setting parts, running prove-outs, troubleshooting fixtures, and generating First Article Inspection documentation on your shop floor.

03
Legacy Programme Optimisation 30–60% cycle reduction typical

We audit slow or unreliable existing routines — restructuring probe paths, approach vectors and speed parameters before recommending any rewrite. Most programmes improve without a full rebuild.

04
FAIR, ISIR & SPC Reporting AS9102 · PPAP · ISO

Ballooned drawing reviews, First Article Inspection Reports to AS9102, Initial Sample Inspection Reports, and SPC datasets — formatted to your customer's exact requirements from the start.

05
Inspection Fixture Design & Build Repeatable part presentation

Bespoke modular fixtures designed and manufactured to eliminate clamping variability. We supply fixture and programme as a single, proven package — one supplier, one point of contact.

06
Training & Operator Cover PC-DMIS · Calypso · MCOSMOS

On-site GD&T and software training from working metrology engineers. Short and long-term CMM operator cover across UK and Ireland manufacturing — no agency fees.

Reasons

What makes
us different —
specifically.

Not mission-statement language. Actual, operational differences that you'll notice from the first conversation.

01

Software-agnostic by design

We have no preferred machine brand to recommend. Hexagon, ZEISS, Mitutoyo, Renishaw, LK — all supported to the same depth. The right answer for your situation is always what we suggest.

02

We fix before we rewrite

Slow legacy programmes usually have a fixable root cause. We audit first — path planning, approach angles, speed parameters — and deliver improvements without the cost or risk of a full rewrite. Typically 30–60% faster.

03

Compliance is built in, not added later

Every programme is structured for AS9100 Rev D, IATF 16949 and ISO 13485 traceability from line one. Reports are AS9102 and PPAP formatted from the start — not retrofitted when your customer asks.

04

Single point of contact across UK & Ireland

One account manager. One technical lead. You call one number whether the job is in Belfast or Bristol. No escalation chains, no ticket systems.

05

Fixture and programme as one package

We design and build inspection fixtures and write the CMM programme for them. No separate suppliers to coordinate — and no ambiguity about who is responsible when something needs adjusting.

06

Engineers answer the phone

Every technical enquiry goes to a qualified metrologist, not a business development manager. If a project isn't within our capability, we say so directly — rather than overpromising scope.
