What we do

Integrated
engineering
solutions.

We bridge the gap between design and production. Our end-to-end engineering services ensure that your manufacturing processes, inspection regimes, and quality management systems are robust, efficient, and fully compliant with industry standards.

01
CNC CMM Programming On-site or offshore · Zero downtime

Offline PC-DMIS programming carried out at your site or remotely. Our team is highly skilled in part setup, inspection planning and CMM programming, ensuring full GD&T compliance. First Article Inspection conducted at your facility or any approved third-party location.

02
On-Site Execution & Prove-Out UK & Ireland deployment

Our engineers embed with your team — setting parts, running prove-outs, troubleshooting fixtures, and generating First Article Inspection documentation on your shop floor.

03
Legacy Programme Optimisation 30–60% cycle reduction typical

We audit slow or unreliable existing routines — restructuring probe paths, approach vectors and speed parameters before recommending any rewrite. Most programmes improve without a full rebuild.

04
FAIR, ISIR & SPC Reporting AS9102 · PPAP · ISO

Ballooned drawing reviews, First Article Inspection Reports to AS9102, Initial Sample Inspection Reports, and SPC datasets — formatted to your customer's exact requirements from the start.

05
Inspection Fixture Design & Build Repeatable part presentation

Bespoke modular fixtures designed and manufactured to eliminate clamping variability. We supply fixture and programme as a single, proven package — one supplier, one point of contact.

06
Training & Operator Cover PC-DMIS · Calypso · MCOSMOS

On-site GD&T and software training from working metrology engineers. Short and long-term CMM operator cover across UK and Ireland manufacturing — no agency fees.

Our Values & Benefits

Built on
integrity, trust
& accountability.

Our mission: delight global customers with value-added, technology-enabled services that achieve quick turnaround times at competitive prices.

01

End-to-end from drawing to finished component

We handle the complete manufacturing journey — from CAD and inspection programming through to finished precision component — so you deal with a single, accountable partner rather than coordinating multiple suppliers.

02

Re-manufacturing through digitisation

Worn-out or legacy parts without drawings are no obstacle. We digitise using advanced 3D laser scanning, CMM and CT scan technology and convert the data into production-ready CAD — making re-manufacturing possible where it would otherwise require expensive tooling redesign.

03

Optimised plant & equipment utilisation

Offline CMM programming means your machines stay productive while inspection programmes are being developed. Faster First Article throughput reduces floor-level bottlenecks and enables quicker production sign-off.

04

Reduced dependency on highly skilled onsite operators

Our remotely deliverable CMM programming service means your production doesn't stop when experienced operators are unavailable. Programmes are delivered ready to run — minimising the skill threshold required on the shop floor.

05

Cost savings across capital, manpower and software

By outsourcing CMM programming to HSPK Aero Ventures, customers avoid the combined cost of software licences, training, and full-time metrology headcount — paying only for the capacity they need, when they need it.
